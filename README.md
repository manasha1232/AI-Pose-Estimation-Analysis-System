---

## 📌 Features

- 🎯 **Real-time 33 Skeletal Keypoint Localization**: Leverages MediaPipe Pose Neural Engine to detect 33 full-body anatomical landmarks in 2D/3D space.
- 🪑 **Ergonomic Workstation Posture Analysis**: Calculates neck slouch angle and torso inclination vectors to identify improper sitting posture and prevent spinal strain.
- 💃 **Kinematic Dance Movement Tracking**: Computes spatial motion vectors and joint trajectories for dynamic performance analytics.
- 🧘 **Autonomous Yoga Pose Classifier**: Evaluates lower limb flexion angles and upper arm spans to classify **Tree Pose (*Vrksasana*)**, **Warrior Pose (*Virabhadrasana*)**, and **T-Pose**.
- 📊 **Pure Side-by-Side Visual Overlay Engine**: Generates high-definition comparative visual feeds (Original Feed vs. Pose Skeleton Overlay).
